# World v2 — the brief

One composition, designed around the words, the lore and the life, instead of features retrofitted
onto the old well. Same engine idea (320×180 art pixels ×6, stepped stone ramps with ordered
dither, light decides the step), same palette family, same creatures.

## The place

You are standing on **the old road**: a wide underground highway of an old people,
long abandoned and taken back by green. You stand on it, facing along it.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ▓▓ CLOCK ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓│ ░sky shaft░ │[icons on lintel]│
│ date · part · temp         │  roots, light falls          │
│                            ╲                               │
│  ▣ WATCH                    ╲   arch  arch  arch  · · ·     │
│  ▣ LISTEN       NEAR WALL    ╲    the road runs away  ◦door │
│                 (calm, dark, ╱   into the dark              │
│  ▣ YOUTUBE       carved)    ╱  side passages left & right   │
│  ▣ APPLE TV                ╱                               │
│  ▣ NETFLIX                ╱     NAME OF THE FOCUSED APP     │
│ ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁ bank / road edge ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁ stream ~~~~~ ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁ │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Near wall (left ~45%)**: face-on dressed stone, darker and quieter than anything else so text
  always reads. The clock is cut into a lintel stone; apps are a column of small carved plaques;
  the focused one is lit by Mote. The tally of days is scratched near the bottom; the old-script
  saying runs down the wall's edge where it meets the road.
- **The road (right ~55%)**: one-point perspective, vanishing point right of centre. Ribbed vault,
  flagstones with grass in the joints, ferns, roots, side passages (one lantern-lit, one with
  mushrooms, one dark). Lit from the shaft and our side; after three or four arches the dark takes
  it. At the end: the door's ember.
- **Sky shaft (top, centre-right)**: a collapse in the vault — the real sky, sun, moon, clouds,
  weather, birds, bats. Daylight falls through it onto the road in a dithered beam.
- **Icon lintel (top right)**: the system icons sit on a dark stone lintel across the road's mouth,
  always on a dark ground.
- **Focused name**: carved large on the road's near side wall, lit, receding with it — part of the
  architecture, not a label.
- **Bank and stream (bottom strip)**: where Pip walks, the frog sits, the mouse runs; a shallow
  stream along the road mirrors everything.

## Light

Exactly one model everywhere: reflectance × light → a step on the graded ramps (stone / warm /
cool / far), Bayer-dithered. Light sources: the sky shaft (follows the hour and weather), Mote,
fireflies, mushrooms, the lantern passage, the door's ember, lamps relit by the weeks. The time of
day grades the ramps. Distance = less light, not haze: the cave goes to true dark.

## Life (always something happening)

Mote (selector, lamp), Pip (dog), the frog (stream), fireflies, a mouse, a spider, a snail, moths
at the lamps, birds/bats via the shaft, fish in the stream, a fox or hare crossing the road far
off, butterflies in the beam by day. They notice each other: Pip chases the mouse, snaps at
fireflies, watches the frog and the Keeper; the frog snaps fireflies; Mote sleeps on Pip.

## The meta layer

The creatures know there is glass. Mote sometimes flies right up to the screen (grows large) and
taps it — a small ring and a tink. Pip sometimes turns and looks straight out at you, head tilted.
When the TV comes on, the well boots from Mote's light; when it goes to sleep, everyone settles.
Rarely, the world glitches for a frame and Pip barks at it.

## Text zones (never covered by life)

Clock + date: top-left. App plaques: left column. Icons: top-right lintel. Focused name: lower right
on the road wall. Hints: bottom-left above the bank. Creatures walk the bank strip and the road,
never across the plaques.
