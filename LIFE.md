# LIFE — who lives on the old road, and how they get along

The world is full; now it should feel *lived in*. Every creature has a place, a day, moods, and knows
the others are there. Nobody wanders into a heap: they keep their distance, give way, and meet on
purpose. This holds in every state: morning to night, rain or clear, music or quiet, festival or not.

## The bank, mapped (home screen, 320 px)


- **Mouse hole** (x≈10, in the foot of the wall): the mouse lives here. Peeks out, darts, nibbles.
- **Pip's bed** (x≈62): where he sleeps (not wherever he happens to stop), his bowl beside it.
- **Open bank** (100–150): Pip's patrol and play ground; festival things are set out here.
- **The rubble** (150–176): the snail's home among the small mushrooms.
- **Road mouth** (176–215): where the channel comes out; Pip drinks here; the way to the Keeper.
- **Frog's stone** (≈222) and the lily pond: the frog sits here, not in the middle of the bank.
- **The grove** (236–300): the big mushrooms; fireflies gather; out of everyone's way.

Other pages keep the same idea on their own stretch of bank.

## Space

Each ground creature claims a little space. Coming near another, the smaller one acts first:
- the **frog** watches Pip come; within ~16 px it plops into the water (ripples; Pip sniffs them);
- the **mouse** never strolls past Pip: it waits at its hole, or darts by while he sleeps;
- the **snail** pulls into its shell when anyone comes close, and stays put a while;
- **Pip** walks around things rather than through them, and never parks on the frog or the decor.
In a party, everyone takes a **dance spot** of their own, spread along the bank.

## Days

| Time       | Pip                                         | Others                                         |
|------------|---------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| Dawn       | stretch, drink at the road mouth            | birds at the opening, dew drips                |
| Morning    | patrols, plays with his bone                | snail out after a damp night, butterflies       |
| Midday     | naps in the shade by the wall               | quiet; the frog sunbathes on its stone          |
| Dusk       | evening zoomies; then supper (bowl filled)  | bats out of the road, frog begins to croak      |
| Night      | sleeps on his bed, Mote curled on his back  | mouse comes out, fireflies, frog chorus        |

**Supper:** every evening his bowl is found filled. Nobody is seen doing it, except on full-moon
nights, when the Keeper comes to the bend and you see the lantern, and Pip runs to the mouth and is
tossed something. (The lore stays quiet: *someone* still feeds him.) He eats, licks the bowl, and is
content (sleepier, slower) for a while.

## Moods (Pip)

- **Energy** — high in the morning, low late at night and after zoomies or a long party; tired
  means slower walks, more lying down, yawns.
- **Fed** — hungry before supper (hangs about his bowl, looks down the road), content after.
- **Missed you** — if the TV's been off a long while, the greeting is bigger: a happy dash to Mote,
  bouncing, tail going; after a short while, just a stretch and a wag.
- **Scared** — thunder: he flattens, ears back, and creeps to his bed; Mote comes and stays by him.
- **Playful** — after a good sleep or when the bone's nearby.

## Weather

- **Rain** — Pip shelters under the road mouth's arch (dry), shakes himself off when it stops; the
  frog comes out and croaks the whole time; the snail comes out; the mouse stays in; no birds.
- **Storm** — Pip hides on his bed, flinches at each flash; the frog loves it.
- **Fog** — everyone slower and closer to home; Pip sniffs more.
- **Snow** — Pip hops at the flakes; the frog stays under.
- **Hot, bright day** — Pip lies in the shade by the wall, panting; nobody hurries.

## Music (Apple Music)

Everyone goes to their dance spot first (no heaps), then dances as their mood allows: Pip dances
less late at night or when tired (lounging, nodding), the snail barely, the mouse a lot. When the
music stops, everyone wanders back home in their own time.

## Quiet Settings

Gets the same world as home, music and party included.
